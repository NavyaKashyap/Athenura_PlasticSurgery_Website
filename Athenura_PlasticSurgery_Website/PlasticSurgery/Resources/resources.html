FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You May Want To Know

Explore answers to common questions about Athenura, our procedures, costs, and what you can expect throughout your aesthetic journey.

Athenura focuses on creating a comfortable and informed experience, where patients can understand their options before making a decision. Each treatment approach is considered according to the individual's goals, needs, and suitability.

Athenura offers a range of surgical and aesthetic procedures designed to address different cosmetic and reconstructive concerns. You can explore the Services section to learn more about the procedures available at the clinic.

Yes, aesthetic care can include both surgical procedures and non-surgical treatments, depending on the patient's concerns and goals. During a consultation, the specialist can explain which approaches may be appropriate for your individual needs.

The choice depends on factors such as your aesthetic goals, the concern being addressed, and the results you are hoping to achieve. A specialist can assess your needs and explain the advantages, limitations, and expected outcomes of suitable options.

The cost varies depending on the procedure, its complexity, the techniques involved, and individual patient requirements. A personalized estimate can be discussed after your consultation and assessment.

Depending on the procedure, additional costs may include consultations, investigations, anesthesia, facility charges, medications, or follow-up care. Your treatment plan and estimated expenses should be discussed with the clinic before proceeding.

The type of anesthesia depends on the specific procedure, its extent, and individual patient considerations. Your specialist and anesthesia team will explain the appropriate approach and what you should expect before the procedure.

Procedure time varies considerably depending on the type and complexity of the surgery being performed. Your specialist can provide a more accurate estimate after evaluating your individual treatment plan.

Scarring can occur after surgical procedures, although its appearance and visibility can vary depending on the procedure and individual healing. Your specialist can explain where scars may be placed and how they are expected to change during healing.

Not every procedure is suitable for every person, and individual health, expectations, medical history, and other factors need to be considered. A proper consultation helps determine whether a particular procedure is appropriate and safe for you.

Depending on the procedure, you may be able to view relevant examples in the clinic's gallery or during your consultation. Examples can help you understand possible outcomes, but individual results can vary from patient to patient.

You can contact Athenura through the contact options provided on the website to enquire about a consultation. During the consultation, you can discuss your concerns, expectations, suitable options, and the estimated treatment expenses.
